# Audience Modes

Choose audience before role. The same facts should be framed differently depending on what the recipient can do.

If the user says only "领导", ask them to choose between `向+1汇报` and `向老板汇报` before drafting. Do not treat "领导" as automatically meaning +1. Provide both versions only when the user explicitly asks for both.

## 向+1汇报

Use for direct managers.

Emphasize:
- Working context: what has been tried, what changed, what is blocked.
- Your judgment: what you think should happen next.
- Operational support: priority, coordination, feedback, resource tradeoff, risk call.
- Ownership: show what you will do before asking for help.

Default structure:

```text
结论：{事项}目前{当前判断}。我建议{你的处理方案}。

当前进展：
- {关键进展/成果}
- {卡点或依赖}

我的判断：
- {为什么这样判断，影响什么}

下一步：
- {你会做什么 + 截止时间}
- {依赖方需要做什么 + 截止时间}

需要您支持：
- {请+1做的具体动作}
```

## 向老板汇报

Use for bosses, senior leaders, skip-level managers, sponsors, or executives.

Emphasize:
- Business impact, not process detail.
- Key facts that change priority, resource, risk, or accountability.
- Recommended option, not just options.
- Decision, authorization, or awareness needed.

Default structure:

```text
结论：{事项}对{业务目标/客户/进度/成本/质量/风险}的影响是{一句话影响}。我的建议是{推荐动作}。

关键事实：
- {最影响判断的事实1}
- {最影响判断的事实2}
- {已采取动作或当前结果}

风险与影响：
- {风险/问题}会导致{具体后果}，最晚需要在{日期}前处理。

建议方案：
- {推荐方案}。理由：{收益/代价/为什么优于其他方案}。

需要您：
- {明确动作}，建议在{日期/时间}前确认。
```

## 跨部门负责人

Use when the recipient controls a dependency but is not in the user's reporting line.

Emphasize:
- Shared goal and dependency.
- Concrete owner/date/action.
- Cost of delay.
- Low-friction ask.

Avoid blaming language. Use "为保证共同目标..." rather than "你们没有...".

## 客户/外部合作方

Use when the recipient is outside the organization.

Emphasize:
- Commitment, timeline, risk control, and next checkpoint.
- What is confirmed vs still being validated.
- Clear asks without exposing internal politics.

Avoid internal jargon, internal owner names unless necessary, and raw uncertainty without a mitigation plan.
